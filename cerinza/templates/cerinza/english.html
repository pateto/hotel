About Cerinza, Boyacá

Cerinza is a beautiful town in Boyacá, surrounded by mountains, lagoons, and natural landscapes. It is perfect for ecotourism, hiking, birdwatching, and enjoying local culture. Main attractions include Laguna del Alto, the colonial church, and the warmth of its people.

Activities in Cerinza

Visit Laguna del Alto

A natural paradise for hiking, picnics, and photography.

Ecological Hiking

Walk through rural landscapes and discover local flora and fauna.

Events & Festivals

Enjoy local culture, music, and gastronomy.
